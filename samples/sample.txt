1. 다음 빈칸에 들어갈 말로 가장 적절한 것은?
When students review their notes right after class, they often feel confident because the material still looks familiar. Yet familiarity is not the same as memory. A week later, many of those students cannot explain the ideas they recognized so easily. Researchers found that the students who closed their notes and tried to recall the lesson from scratch remembered far more, even though the practice felt harder and slower. The struggle to pull information out of memory is exactly what makes it stay. In short, learning lasts longer when we ______.
① read the same notes again and again
② study only when we feel confident
③ make ourselves retrieve what we learned
④ highlight every important sentence
⑤ review immediately after every class
정답: ③
